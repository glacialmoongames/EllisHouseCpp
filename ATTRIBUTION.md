# Attribution and AI disclosure

**Elli's House** was originally created in GameMaker by the **Glacial Moon Games
team** for **GameJaaj 7**.

Original in-game credits:

- Programming: Annie
- Graphics: Pavão Gripado and IGustaMe
- Music: BainoLOL

The Windows C++, Game Boy Advance, Nintendo 3DS and PSP ports were developed
with AI assistance. The AI-assisted work includes conversion, platform backend
implementation, optimization, packaging and documentation. The project remains
a Glacial Moon Games work; AI assistance does not claim authorship of the
original game, artwork, music or design.

The original GameMaker version published on itch.io was created by the Glacial
Moon Games team without AI assistance. The disclosure above applies only to the
later ports contained in this repository.
